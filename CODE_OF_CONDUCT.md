# Community code of conduct

GenyCaster welcomes people who want to improve open, secure and accessible social software. We expect respectful, technically constructive interaction in issues, pull requests, code reviews and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Critique implementations and evidence, not people.
- Be considerate of differing experience levels, languages, accessibility needs and backgrounds.
- Provide reproducible reports; distinguish observations, hypotheses and proven defects.
- Accept technical feedback, explain disagreement respectfully and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect contributors' privacy, ownership of their work, and relevant third-party licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or degrading language, doxxing, deliberate disruption, repeated personal attacks and publishing private credentials or user data are not permitted.

Technical disagreements, security reviews and direct discussion of regressions are welcome when expressed professionally.

## Reporting concerns

Use the repository's GitHub moderation/reporting controls for harmful content or behavior. You may also open a non-sensitive issue requesting maintainer moderation; do not expose another person's private information in that issue.

**Security vulnerabilities are different from conduct reports:** disclose them privately following [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through public discussion.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, request a change in behavior, or restrict participation to protect project collaboration. They should apply these expectations consistently and explain moderation actions where appropriate.

This policy applies to project-maintained public spaces. It does not override GitHub's own terms or conduct rules.
